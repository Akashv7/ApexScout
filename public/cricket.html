SPORT: CRICKET

Ground vs Turf are scored separately —
on purpose.

Leather-ball ground cricket and tennis-ball turf/box cricket reward different skills and attract different competition levels. Mixing them into one leaderboard would make the Talent Index meaningless, so each format gets its own board and its own league-strength model.

Ground / Leather-ball
Turf / Box / Tennis-ball
# Player League League Strength Talent Index

League Strength is a live Elo-style rating (0–100) computed from match results within that league — it's what the Talent Index Engine adjusts against, so a strong performance in a weak league doesn't outrank a strong performance in a tough one without good reason.
