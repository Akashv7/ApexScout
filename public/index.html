WHY THIS EXISTS

Raw stats lie. Context doesn't.

The problem

A batter averaging 60 in a weak league looks identical, on paper, to one averaging 60 in a brutal one. Selectors have no way to tell the difference — so they default to ignoring local leagues entirely.

The fix

Every match feeds a real Elo-style league/team strength rating. Individual stats are weighted by who you actually beat — computed live by the Talent Index Engine, not typed in by hand.

The result

Players dominant in a weaker league get flagged by the Discovery Agent as worth a closer look, rather than dismissed for playing "easy" cricket.
